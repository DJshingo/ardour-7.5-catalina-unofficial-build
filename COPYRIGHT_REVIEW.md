## Adopted attribution and alternatives

The publisher chose the **multiple-contributors** option. The other formats below are retained only as guidance if later evidence requires a correction:

| Option | Suitable when | MIT copyright notice format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual | One person owns the original notes, scripts and other MIT-covered content | `Copyright (c) 2026 <legal name or clearly attributable chosen public identity>` |
| Organization | An employer/organization owns the relevant rights, including any employer-assigned works | `Copyright (c) 2026 <organization name>` |
| Multiple contributors | Each rights holder authorized this distribution and the group designation accurately identifies them | `Copyright (c) 2026 Contributors to the Ardour 7.5 Catalina Build Notes` or a list of agreed names |

If rights are held by different people or organizations, consult them and preserve their notices. Do not assume a GitHub username, repository administrator, employer, or person operating the Mac owns all the content merely because of that role. Do not use the collective notice to conceal an ownership dispute or missing permissions.
